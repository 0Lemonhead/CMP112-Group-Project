# CMP112-Group-Project
The group unity project for CMP112

The game will be a simple twinstick tanks game with enemies that roam until the player enterstheir field of view, they will then begin to attack the player and the player can kill the enemies to increase their score

The tanks should be made of 2 main parts, the base and the cannon. The base rotates with the tanks movement direction and the cannon rotates where the the tank is aiming.

Potential ideas:
  Level system - Killing enemies grants the player xp that can be used to upgrade fire rate, weapon damage or movement speed.
  Enemy level - Each enemy will have random stats assigned when they are instantiated and a level indicator based on their stat levels.
